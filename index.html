About the Author

Rajat Mehrotra is a corporate leader, author, speaker, and above all, a lifelong learner[cite: 11]. With years of experience navigating the corporate world, he has come to believe that some of life’s most meaningful lessons are learned not in boardrooms, but through relationships, setbacks, personal reflection, and the courage to begin again[cite: 11].

Deeply committed to his family, Rajat continues to discover what it means to grow as a father, a husband, and an individual[cite: 11]. A self-proclaimed "survival cook," he finds unexpected parallels between managing a kitchen and leading in the corporate world[cite: 11]. Both demand adaptability, patience, problem-solving, and the ability to work with what life puts on the table[cite: 11].

Writing is not his primary profession, but observing life is second nature to him[cite: 11]. His stories draw inspiration from real experiences, personal mistakes, ambitions, vulnerabilities, and the gradual understanding that comes with personal growth[cite: 11].

Debut Book

The Heart that Restarted

A deeply personal, reflective story about life's unexpected pauses, the fragility of relationships, and the possibility of rediscovering what truly matters[cite: 13]. Through Kabir and Rhea, the narrative explores love, ambition, vulnerability, mistakes, and the quiet courage required to start over[cite: 13].

At its heart, the book is a reminder that life's interruptions can become opportunities for transformation—and that sometimes, losing our rhythm helps us find our way back to ourselves[cite: 13].
