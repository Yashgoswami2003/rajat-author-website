About the Author

Rajat Mehrotra is a corporate leader, author, speaker, and above all, a lifelong learner. With years of experience navigating the corporate world, he has come to believe that some of life's most meaningful lessons are learned not in boardrooms, but through relationships, setbacks, personal reflection, and the courage to begin again.

Deeply committed to his family, Rajat continues to discover what it means to grow as a father, a husband, and an individual. A self-proclaimed "survival cook," he finds unexpected parallels between managing a kitchen and leading in the corporate world.

Philosophy & Vision

"Writing is the art of pausing time to examine the heartbeat of human experience."

Through books, keynote speaking, and thoughtful reflections, Rajat aims to spark conversations around vulnerability, emotional healing, and purposeful leadership.
